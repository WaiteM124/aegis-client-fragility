## Modelling choices that use derived ratios

These are arithmetic on cited inputs, not new evidence:

1. Geographic and segment **shares** = stated £m / £5,214.2m.
2. **EPF vs direct** UK supply split = 60% / 40% of UK geographic share (ar-2015 × fy-results-2016).
3. EPF split support vs construction ≈ support/(support+construction ex-ME) within UK activity stub (segment mix proxy) — see GAPS / low-confidence list.
4. **liquidity_shock WOR** = average−spot net borrowing / (cash + undrawn bank).
5. **me_order_book_shock WOR** = relative fall in ME orders+probable.
